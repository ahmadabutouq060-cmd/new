====================================================
PRODUCT CONCEPT — NASEEJ
====================================================

NASEEJ is a Jordanian tourism experience built around:

STORY + MYSTERY + DISCOVERY + CHOICE + AI + GAMIFICATION

NASEEJ is NOT a normal tourism directory.

The user does not simply search for:
"Where should I go?"

Instead, the user asks:

"What story do I want to discover?"

The user chooses a STORY THREAD.

A thread begins with a mystery, question, or incomplete story.

The user then:
- discovers a place
- visits a real location
- observes something around them
- solves a challenge
- receives a clue
- earns ATHAR points
- unlocks the next chapter
- makes choices
- follows a new part of the story
- eventually reaches a final reveal

CORE EXPERIENCE:

Choose a Thread
→ Discover
→ Visit
→ Observe
→ Solve
→ Earn ATHAR
→ Unlock
→ Choose the Next Chapter
→ Reveal the Story

The key emotional goal is:

MAKE THE USER CURIOUS ENOUGH TO KEEP GOING.

NASEEJ should feel like:

"A story you travel through."

Not:

"A list of places."

====================================================
THE CORE DIFFERENTIATOR
====================================================

The website must communicate this idea:

"NASEEJ does not tell you where to go.
It gives you a story to follow."

The user should feel that every location is a CHAPTER rather than simply a destination.

Every chapter should reveal only part of the story.

Do not reveal everything at once.

Use:
- clues
- mystery
- locked chapters
- partial information
- meaningful choices
- progress
- rewards
- story reveals

====================================================
THE THREAD SYSTEM
====================================================

The THREAD is the central product metaphor.

A thread is a story that connects:

places
people
objects
culture
food
craft
history
memories
experiences

The thread should NOT simply be a straight timeline.

It can branch.

Example:

Chapter 01
Tree

↓
Chapter 02
Person

↓
Choice:

Follow the Person
OR
Follow the Object

Then the story branches.

The user's choices should determine which chapter appears next.

This makes every journey feel personal.

The final reveal should show how the places, clues, people, and objects were connected.
